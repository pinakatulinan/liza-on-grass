# liza-on-grass
My mother's sante barley e commerce web-app with AI integration.

## Prototype

`prototype/index.html` is a clickable design prototype of the storefront (single HTML file):

- Home, shop, product pages with live Three.js 3D product models and an AI benefits guide
- Cart, checkout with GCash / Maya (receipt upload) and cash on delivery, zone-based shipping, promo codes
- Order confirmation, order tracking, optional customer accounts
- Owner dashboard preview (orders, payment verification, prices and stock, shipping zones, promos)
- English / Filipino toggle, light and dark themes, phone layout with bottom tabs

Products, prices, account numbers and contact details are placeholders until the real list is supplied.
